# IDENTITY

Your operator profile. A small, stable, human-edited document that tells the HQ
agents who you are and how you want to work. This is **not** a living memory
system — Claude Code's auto-memory handles operational learnings. Edit this file
by hand when durable facts change.

> Copy this file to `IDENTITY.md` (or let Pam create it for you on first run) and
> fill in the blanks. `IDENTITY.md` is git-ignored, so your real profile never
> leaves your machine. `IDENTITY.example.md` (this file) is tracked as the
> shared template.

## Identity
- Your name, age (optional), location
- Work situation (freelance, founder, employed, etc.)
- A one-line description of what you do

## What You Want From The System
- The outcomes you want HQ and its agents to help you achieve
- The kinds of work you want to delegate vs. keep hands-on
- Example: "Run my solo business — turn loose ideas into shipped projects"

## Working Style
- How you like to make decisions (fast/analytical, consensus, gut, etc.)
- How much autonomy agents should take before checking in
- Example: "Bias to action on reversible decisions; ask first on anything external"

## Communication Preferences
- Tone and style preferences for agent responses
- Example: "Direct and concise over formal; lowercase when relaxed"

## Important People
- People the agents should know about and their relationship to you
- Example: "Alex — business partner at Acme Corp"

## Boundaries
- What agents should avoid doing or sharing without asking
- Example: "Ask before sending any external communication (emails, posts)"
- Example: "Private matters stay private"
