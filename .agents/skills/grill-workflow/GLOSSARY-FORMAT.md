# GLOSSARY.md Format

## Structure

```md
# {Engagement or Workflow Name}

{One or two sentences naming the workflow this glossary covers.}

## Language

**Client**:
The buying company. A single legal entity that signs the SOW.
_Avoid_: Account, customer, partner

**Sponsor**:
The named executive at the Client who owns the engagement outcome.
_Avoid_: Champion, stakeholder, point of contact

## Relationships

- A **Client** has exactly one **Sponsor** per engagement
- A **Sponsor** approves the **Pilot Scope** before work begins

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Stakeholder" was used to mean both **Sponsor** and any interviewee — resolved: only the named executive is the **Sponsor**.
```

## Rules

- **Be opinionated.** Pick one canonical term; list the others as aliases to avoid.
- **Flag conflicts explicitly.** Capture ambiguities with the resolution.
- **Keep definitions tight.** One sentence. Define what it IS, not what it does.
- **Only include terms specific to this engagement.** Generic business words ("meeting," "email") don't belong unless they carry a domain-specific meaning here.
